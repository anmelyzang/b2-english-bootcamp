# W1-1 范文与写作思路拆解

同一道题目：*Should schools focus only on academic subjects, or should they also teach practical life skills? Write about: (1) academic subjects (2) practical life skills (3) your own idea.* 下面这篇范文刻意避开了你自己作文里出现过的每一种错误（SVA1/T1/W1/SYN1/DET1），读的时候可以逐句对照。

## 范文全文（186词）

**① 引言**
Should schools teach only academic subjects, or should practical life skills also have a place in the curriculum? In my view, both are equally important, and neither should be sacrificed for the other.

**② 论点一：学术科目**
Academic subjects clearly form the foundation of a good education. Without a solid understanding of science, for instance, students would struggle to pursue careers such as medicine. A doctor who does not understand chemistry could easily make a dangerous mistake when prescribing medicine.

**③ 论点二：生活技能**
However, academic knowledge alone does not prepare students for everyday life. Skills such as cooking, managing money and communicating effectively are rarely taught in lessons, yet they are essential for independence. A graduate who has never learned to manage a budget may quickly fall into debt after leaving home.

**④ 论点三：我的观点**
In my opinion, schools should therefore combine both types of learning rather than treating them as separate options. A short weekly class on practical skills would help students build confidence without lowering academic standards.

**⑤ 结论**
In conclusion, academic subjects and life skills serve different but equally valuable purposes. Schools should aim to teach both, so students leave education both knowledgeable and capable.

## 一、段落间逻辑（宏观结构——为什么是这五段，顺序为什么这样排）

| 段落   | 作用                                                                   | 和上一段的连接方式                            |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| ①引言  | 把题目改写成一句问句+一句表态，让读者一开始就知道"我站哪一边"                                     | —（开头）                                |
| ②论点一 | 先讲题目给的第一个要点（学术科目），因为这是"默认立场"，先立稳一个论点                                 | 直接展开，不需要转折词                          |
| ③论点二 | 讲题目给的第二个要点（生活技能），用**However**开头，明确signal"这是另一面"                      | However = 语气转折，告诉读者"接下来要讲不同的角度"      |

## 二、逐句写作逻辑（微观——每一句在段落里的具体任务）

### 论点一段（3句）

1. *"Academic subjects clearly form the foundation of a good education."* —— **主题句**，先给结论，不给例子。读者读完这一句就知道这段在论证什么。
2. *"Without a solid understanding of science, for instance, students would struggle to pursue careers such as medicine."* —— **展开一层**：从"基础"这个抽象说法，具体到"没学好科学→没法做某些职业"。
3. *"A doctor who does not understand chemistry could easily make a dangerous mistake when prescribing medicine."* —— **给出证据**：一个具体到"人物+后果"的例子，让论点一有真实的支撑（对应E1错误代码：论点必须配证据）。

### 论点二段（3句）

1. *"However, academic knowledge alone does not prepare students for everyday life."* —— **转折主题句**：However先示意"要讲另一面"，然后立刻给出这一段的论点。
2. *"Skills such as cooking, managing money and communicating effectively are rarely taught in lessons, yet they are essential for independence."* —— **列举+让步**：先列举三个具体技能（不是空泛地说"life skills"），yet再次强调"虽然没人教，但很重要"。
3. *"A graduate who has never learned to manage a budget may quickly fall into debt after leaving home."* —— **同款证据句型**：和论点一第3句用的是同一个模具（"A [身份] who does not/has never [缺陷] may/could [后果]"），刻意重复这个句型，让两段的论证力度对称。

### 论点三段（2句，题目要求的"你自己的想法"）

1. *"In my opinion, schools should therefore combine both types of learning rather than treating them as separate options."* —— **给出立场**：therefore连接前两段（因为①②都重要，所以……），rather than明确否定"二选一"这个题目暗示的对立框架。
2. *"A short weekly class on practical skills would help students build confidence without lowering academic standards."* —— **落地成具体建议**：不是空喊口号，给一个"怎么做"的具体方案（每周一节课），并且预先堵住反对意见（不会降低学术标准）。

### 结论段（2句）

1. *"In conclusion, academic subjects and life skills serve different but equally valuable purposes."* —— 把①②两段各用几个词概括，呼应而不是重复。
2. *"Schools should aim to teach both, so students leave education both knowledgeable and capable."* —— 回到引言"两者都重要"的立场，首尾呼应。
